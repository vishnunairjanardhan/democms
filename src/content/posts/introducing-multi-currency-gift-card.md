## Why Every Global Shopify Merchant Needs Multi-Currency Gift Cards

Selling globally on Shopify or Shopify Plus opens up massive opportunities. But it only works if your customer experience matches global expectations. Currency confusion is a major reason why international shoppers abandon their carts or hesitate to complete a purchase.

Here’s why every serious Shopify merchant should be using multi-currency gift cards:

- <b>Eliminate Checkout Friction</b>: Avoid abandoned carts by letting customers redeem gift cards in their local currency. A familiar, localized experience builds trust and speeds up the path to purchase.

- <b>Unlock Global Gifting Potential</b>: Multi-currency gift cards make it easy for customers to send digital gifts across borders without worrying about currency mismatches. Whether it's gifting during Lunar New Year in Singapore or Christmas in Germany, you’re ready for it all.

- <b>Reduce Cart Abandonment</b>: Currency mismatches create uncertainty. Multi-currency gift cards provide clarity and confidence, reducing drop-offs during the final step of checkout.

- <b>Increase Sales During Key Gifting Seasons</b>: Gift-heavy moments are global. With support for local currencies, you are positioned to maximize revenue during holidays, seasonal campaigns, and special promotions. It doesn’t matter where your customer is shopping from.
