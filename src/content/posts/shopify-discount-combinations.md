<h2 id="What-is-Shopify’s-Discount-Combination-Feature?">What is Shopify’s Discount Combination Feature?</h2>

Shopify allows store owners to create different types of discounts that customers can use at checkout. These discounts help increase sales, attract new buyers, and reward loyal customers.
Some common types of discounts in Shopify include:

<ul role= "list">
<li><b>Percentage discounts:</b> A discount that gives a percentage off the total price (e.g., 10% off all products).</li>
<li><b>Fixed-amount discounts:</b> A discount that removes a fixed dollar amount (e.g., $5 off a specific item).</li>
<li><b>Free shipping discounts:</b> A discount that removes the shipping fee.</b>
<li><b>Buy X Get Y discounts:</b> A promotion where a customer gets a free or discounted item when they buy a specific product (e.g., Buy 1 Get 1 Free).</li>
</ul>
While Shopify allows customers to use some discounts together, there are still several restrictions that limit flexibility.

<h2 id="Types-of-Discounts-That-Can-Be-Combined">Types of Discounts That Can Be Combined</h2>

Shopify does allow a few discount combinations, but with strict limitations. The following discount types can be stacked:
<ul role= "list">
<li><b>Percentage-based discount + Free shipping:</b> Customers can apply both a percentage discount (e.g., 20% off) and a free shipping discount in the same order.</li>
<li><b>Fixed-amount discount + Free shipping:</b> Customers can use both a fixed discount (e.g., $10 off) and a free shipping discount together.</li>
<li><b>Buy X Get Y + Free shipping:</b> A customer can apply a Buy X Get Y discount and also get free shipping.</li>
</ul>

While these combinations help, they are not enough for most merchants who want more flexibility in discount stacking.

<h2 id="Shopify’s-Limitations-on-Discount-Stacking">Shopify’s Limitations on Discount Stacking</h2>

Even though Shopify allows some discount stacking, there are still several major limitations:
Only one discount code can be applied per order: Customers cannot use multiple coupon codes in the same purchase.

<ul role= "list">
<li><b>Only one automatic discount per order:</b> If a store has an automatic discount (e.g., "10% off orders over $100"), no other discount can be applied.</li>
<li><b>Limited support for stacking gift cards and discount codes:</b> Customers cannot easily use a gift card along with a discount code.</li>
<li><b>No advanced discount rules:</b> Merchants cannot create custom rules for applying discounts based on cart value, product category, or purchase history.</li>
</ul>

These restrictions make it difficult for merchants to provide personalized discounts, leading to frustrated customers and lost sales opportunities.
