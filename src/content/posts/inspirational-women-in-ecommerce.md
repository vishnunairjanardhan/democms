Join 99minds in celebrating women in eCommerce who are driving the industry forward with their incomparable contributions. Let us look at a few of them who have made a name for themselves, either as entrepreneurs or employees.

<h2>Lillian Tung</h2>

She’s the co-founder and CMO of Fur, a body-care line that promotes natural beauty. The haircare brand started with her co-founder Laura Schubert is running in its 6th year now. Tung is a graduate of Columbia Business School, from where she did her MBA.

<h2>Trinity Mouzon Wofford</h2>

She is the co-founder and CEO of Golde, a superfood health and beauty brand. Trinity built Golde around the concept of making wellness approachable, fun, and inclusive. Golde’s suite of products is available at over 100 retailers. She also became the youngest black woman to launch a product line in Sephora. In 2019, Inc Magazine named her as one of the top 100 female founders.

<h2>Lauren Bosworth</h2>

She was once a regular at reality TV shows on MTV before she jumped into the entrepreneurship bandwagon. Bosworth has created an all-inclusive feminine health brand called Love Wellness. She founded the company in 2016 with a range of products that consist of natural and organic raw materials. Her advisory board consists of medical doctors and nutritionists.

<h2>Helena Price Hambrecht</h2>

She worked in branding, marketing, and communications in Silicon Valley before starting up with Haus. Her company makes drinks or aperitifs. Their drinks are made with real fruits, herbs, botanicals, and so on. They do not even add artificial ingredients or refined sugar to their products.

<h2>Alexandra Tanner</h2>

Currently, the AVP of Digital Marketing & Ecommerce at L’Oreal has risen from the ranks. She has been passionate about eCommerce and digital marketing and has more than 14 plus years of direct-to-consumer brand experience. These women are emblematic of hard-work and have the passion and pride required to change the face of eCommerce.

While there are many more inspirational women in eCommerce across the world, if you are looking for some inspiration to unearth the hidden gem inside you, then you can check out these women.
