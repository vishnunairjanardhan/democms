<h2 id="1">Benefits of Cross-Platform Gift Card Integration</h2>

A gift card is not just a gift option; it is much more than that. It helps businesses increase repeat purchases and revenue. Here’s why integrating gift cards across Lightspeed X-Series and BigCommerce is a game-changer:

### 1. Seamless Omnichannel Experience

Customers can purchase a gift card on your BigCommerce storefront and redeem it in your physical store powered by Lightspeed X-Series, or vice versa. This seamless experience increases <a href="https://www.99minds.io/blog/customer-retention" role="link" target="_blanck">customer retention</a> and smoothens the customer buying journey. 

### 2. Increased Sales Opportunities

A gift card recipient is a potential new customer. Once they enter your store or site, they often spend more than the card’s value. By offering customers the option to use a gift card both online and in a physical store, you eliminate barriers that might stop them from converting.

### 3. Streamlined Operations

With centralized gift card data and transaction syncing across platforms, you eliminate the need for manual reconciliation or duplicate systems. That means fewer errors and better reporting.

### 4. Better Customer Loyalty

Gift cards are a great gateway into <a href="https://www.99minds.io/blog/loyalty-program" role="link" target="_blanck">loyalty programs</a>. When integrated with platforms like 99minds, you can track customer behavior and incentivize future purchases through points, cashback, and promotions.

<h2 id="2">Selling and Accepting Gift Cards in Lightspeed Retail (X-Series) POS</h2>

Lightspeed Retail (X-Series), formerly known as Vend, is a popular POS system for modern retail stores. With the help of 99minds, you can sell and redeem gift cards directly from the POS, making in-store gift card management easy.

<b>How It Works</b>

- <b>Selling a Gift Card</b>: When a customer buys a gift card, your staff can issue one directly from the Lightspeed retail POS. The card details are securely stored in Lightspeed Retail and Big Commerce, and the customer receives a digital or physical version (depending on your setup).

- <b>Redeeming a Gift Card</b>: At checkout, your staff selects "Gift Card" as the payment method and enters the card code. The system automatically checks the balance and deducts the amount for both Lightspeed and BigCommerce.

By using 99minds with Lightspeed, all gift card activity syncs with your broader retail operations, making life easier for your staff and ensuring a smooth customer experience.

<h2 id="3">Selling and Accepting Gift Cards on BigCommerce</h2>

When a customer buys a gift card online, 99minds creates a gift card code that works with both BigCommerce and Lightspeed X-Series. There are two ways to sell gift cards online:

### 1. Through the BigCommerce Product Page

Customers can also buy gift cards digitally through your BigCommerce store using the 99minds widget:

</li>

<li>

</li>

<li>

The recipient receives the <a href="https://www.99minds.io/blog/a-comprehensive-guide-to-digital-gift-cards" role="link" target="_blanck">digital gift card</a> via email or SMS, powered by 99minds.

</ul>

These gift cards are securely stored in the 99minds system, linked to the recipient’s phone number or email. They are instantly redeemable in-store via Lightspeed X or online through BigCommerce, thanks to the seamless 99minds integration between both platforms.

### 2. Through the 99minds Gift Card Storefront Embedded in BigCommerce
This option allows customers to personalize the gift card during checkout. Payment goes directly to your connected payment account.
