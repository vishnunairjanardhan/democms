Creating a smooth and unified customer experience is crucial in today's omnichannel retail landscape. That’s where 99minds steps in—empowering merchants to effortlessly sell and redeem gift cards in-store and online through powerful integrations with Lightspeed Retail (X-Series) and Shopify.

Whether your customer is walking into a physical store or shopping online, 99minds ensures that gift cards are always a convenient and versatile option. Let’s break down how it all works.

<h2 id="Lightspeed-Retail-(X-Series)-+-99minds:-Sell-&-Redeem-with-Ease">Lightspeed Retail (X-Series) + 99minds: Sell & Redeem with Ease</h2>
<h3>Selling a Gift Card In-Store</h3>
With 99minds integrated into Lightspeed Retail (X-Series), selling a physical gift card becomes as straightforward as any other product on your shelf:
<ol>
<li><strong> Start the Sale:</strong> Navigate to the Sell screen via the Lightspeed left-hand menu.</li>
<li><strong> Find the Product:</strong> Search for “Gift Card” in the product search bar.</li>
<li><strong> Add the Card:</strong> On the Add New Gift Card screen, select the gift card product and enter the customer’s gift card number.</li>
<li><strong> Verify & Load:</strong> Click Find Gift Card to validate the card, then enter the amount the customer wants to load.
</li>
<li><strong> Complete the Sale:</strong> Click Add to Sale and finish the transaction as you normally would.</li>
</ol>


The transaction details are instantly recorded in the 99minds platform under the Recent Transactions section, giving merchants real-time visibility and control.

<h3>Redeeming a Gift Card In-Store</h3>
When a customer wants to redeem a gift card in-store:
<ol>
<li><strong> Choose Gift Card as Payment Method:</strong> During checkout, select Gift Card as the payment type.</li>
<li><strong> Scan or Enter the Number:</strong> Input the gift card number and click Find Gift Card.</li>
<li><strong> Process Payment:</strong> Finish the transaction and click Pay with Gift Card. The amount will be deducted from the card balance automatically.</li>

</ol>

<h2 id="Redemption-Made-Simple-on-Shopify">Redemption Made Simple on Shopify</h2>
Gift cards issued in-store can also be used online, thanks to 99minds' cross-platform integration with major eCommerce platforms like Shopify.

Here’s how customers redeem gift cards online:

<ol>
<li><strong> Checkout:</strong> At the Order Summary page, customers click Gift Certificate.</li>
<li><strong> Apply the Code:</strong> They enter their unique gift card code and click Apply.</li>
<li><strong> Pay Balance:</strong> If the gift card doesn’t cover the full amount, you can pay the remaining balance using another method</li>

</ol>

This fluid omnichannel experience ensures that gift cards work wherever your customers choose to shop, giving your brand greater reach, flexibility, and customer satisfaction.
