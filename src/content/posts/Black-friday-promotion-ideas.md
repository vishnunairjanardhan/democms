<h2 id="20-Black-Friday-Promotion-Ideas-that-make-your-offer-tempting">20 Black Friday Promotion Ideas that make your offer tempting</h2>

Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals are highly competitive. With nearly every brand offering their best discounts during the same four days, it can feel overwhelming trying to stand out. 

So, how can you ensure your brand grabs attention in the midst of all the holiday noise?

Check out these 20 creative black friday marketing ideas for 2025 that are guaranteed to increase your sales.

<ol>
<li>
<h3>Create a Loyalty Program</h3>

Creating a <a href="https://www.99minds.io/blog/loyalty-program">Loyalty Program</a> is an excellent Black Friday promotion idea to increase <a href="https://www.99minds.io/blog/customer-retention">customer retention</a> and encourage repeat purchases. 

By rewarding loyal customers with exclusive discounts or early access to Black Friday sales ideas, you can foster long-term brand engagement. Offering points for purchases or special Black Friday rewards helps create a sense of exclusivity and excitement.

Using <a href="https://www.99minds.io/">99minds</a>, you can easily build a personalized loyalty program that fits your brand’s goals, allowing you to automate and manage your loyalty strategies effectively throughout the year.
</li>

<li>
<h3>Offer a sneak peek</h3>

Teasing your Black Friday deals can spark curiosity and build excitement. 

If you run an eCommerce store, consider offering a sneak peek of some top products that will be on sale. For SaaS businesses, sharing a brief video or GIF showcasing key features of your software can create buzz. 

Be sure to emphasize that these previews are part of your Black Friday promotion ideas and highlight the impressive discounts that will follow. Framing the sneak peek within your Black Friday marketing ideas helps build anticipation and drive traffic as the big day approaches.

Here are 9 creative ways to promote your sneak peeks:
<ul>
<li>Send an email blast.</li>
<li>Promote it across your social media platforms.</li>
<li>Publish a YouTube teaser video.</li>
<li>Add engaging pop-ups to your website.</li>
<li>Create targeted Search and Display ads.</li>
<li>Share it via a radio or podcast commercial.</li>
<li>Distribute flyers to spread the word.</li>
<li>If you’re a larger business, consider TV or billboard ads.</li>
<li>Feature it in your newsletter.</li>
</ul>

</li>

<li>
<h3>Unveil a New Product</h3>

Black Friday is more than just discounts—it’s about standing out with unique offerings. This year, consider launching a Black Friday-exclusive product to create buzz and urgency.

Offering something only available during Black Friday sales taps into customers' fear of missing out, compelling them to act fast. By presenting a fresh, limited-time item, you position your brand ahead of the competition. 

Whether it’s a new product launch or a limited edition, this Black Friday promotion idea can help set your sales event apart and drive conversions.

Here are some examples of exclusive products you can introduce or release on Black Friday to captivate your customers:

<ul>
<li>Limited edition gadgets with unique designs or colors only available during Black Friday.</li>
<li>Collaborative fashion lines featuring artists or influencers, exclusive to the shopping event.</li>
<li>Special product bundles with significant discounts when purchased together.</li>
<li>Customizable gift sets tailored specifically for the Black Friday crowd.</li>
<li>Exclusive jewelry or collector’s pieces available for a limited time.</li>
<li>Personalized experiences, like invitations to future events or sneak peeks into new product lines.</li>
</ul>
</li>

<li>
<h3>Take a Spin and Win Big!</h3>

You can gamify your Black Friday promotion ideas to enhance <a href="https://www.99minds.io/blog/customer-engagement" role="link" target="_blanck">customer engagement</a>. A popular approach is to incorporate a virtual spin-the-wheel feature on your website. 

This interactive element entices visitors by offering a chance to win discounts, prizes, or coupons. While the focus may not always be on the rewards, the fun and engaging experience it provides boosts traffic and creates a positive brand association

To make the most of Black Friday sale ideas with a virtual wheel, follow these tips:

<ul>
<li>Add social sharing options to let customers promote their wins and organically spread the word.</li>
<li>Keep the wheel design simple and intuitive for a smooth user experience.</li>
<li>Offer a range of small to large prizes to engage a variety of customers and boost participation.</li>
<li>Clearly communicate rules and prize conditions upfront to avoid confusion.</li>
<li>Use bright colors and festive graphics to capture attention and create a celebratory Black Friday vibe.</li>
<li>Personalized experiences, like invitations to future events or sneak peeks into new product lines.</li>
</ul>
</li>

<img loading="lazy" role="img" src="/assets/blog/spin-to-win6.webp" alt="spin-and-win-black-friday">

<li>
<h3>Offer a VIP Early-Bird Discount</h3>

The early bird truly gets the best deal with Black Friday! Start by using your email list to notify your VIP customers about Black Friday promotion ideas, offering early access to discounted products. 

Create urgency by using phrases like "limited availability" or "almost sold out," tapping into the psychology of scarcity to prompt quicker decisions. 

Studies show that fear of missing out (FOMO) plays a huge role, with 40% of shoppers feeling frustrated when they miss Black Friday deals—proving the effectiveness of limited-time offers.
</li>

<li>
<h3>Create a Countdown Promo Video</h3>

Another great tactic is to create a promotional video with a countdown timer, highlighting the upcoming Black Friday deals. 

You can feature a selection of the items on sale, building anticipation a week in advance. This approach not only generates excitement but also encourages customers to plan their Black Friday shopping in advance. 

Once your promo video is ready, be sure to promote it on your Instagram or other social media platforms to maximize reach and engagement.
</li>

<li>
<h3>Collab with Tiktok Influencers</h3>

TikTok has become a powerful platform for Black Friday promotion ideas, especially when reaching younger demographics like Millennials and Gen Z. By partnering with influencers who consistently gather millions of views, you can tap into this platform’s vast audience. 

When selecting the right influencer, ensure their style and followers align with your brand's identity. Launch creative Black Friday marketing ideas by encouraging viewers to "swipe up" for exclusive deals. 

Combining a well-matched influencer with enticing offers can create a highly effective Black Friday campaign that drives significant traffic and sales.
</li>

<li>
<h3>Offer Free Gifts with Every Purchase</h3>

Who doesn’t love freebies? Offering a free gift with every purchase is a proven Black Friday promotion idea that encourages customers to spend more.

Small businesses often offer inexpensive items, like products under $2, with purchases, or set a minimum order requirement like $75 for the free gift. To maximize visibility, highlight this promotion on your store’s homepage. 

Although it may seem like an old tactic, research and major brands show that gift-with-purchase campaigns remain an effective Black Friday marketing idea for driving sales and building customer satisfaction.

</li>
<img loading="lazy" role="img" src="/assets/blog/Estee+Lauder free gift.jpeg" style="width: 800px; height: 600px;" alt="free-gift-with-purchase">

<li>
<h3>Offer a Buy-One, Get-One 50% Off</h3>

This strategy focuses on encouraging customers to spend more by creating irresistible deals. A great variation could be to offer different promotions throughout the day on Black Friday. 

For instance, you could start with a 50% off everything sale in the morning, followed by a "Buy One, Get One 50% Off" deal in the afternoon. 

This shifting promotion tactic can increase the urgency and anticipation, motivating shoppers to purchase more throughout the day, making it a highly effective Black Friday marketing idea.
</li>

<li>
<h3> Offer Bundle Deals</h3>

Bundling products together can be a great Black Friday promotion idea to boost sales, as customers perceive higher value by purchasing items as a package deal. For instance, offering a special bundle price for products that are often bought together, such as a beauty kit or a tech bundle, can make the deal irresistible. 

Emphasize the savings compared to purchasing items separately. Whether it's buy-one-get-one-50%-off or a bundle deal, ensure the products are complementary to maintain the perceived value and effectiveness of the Black Friday sale idea.

For example, you can create compelling Black Friday promotion ideas by bundling or discounting items such as:

Complementary skincare products, such as cleansers and moisturizers, in a single package.
Matching clothing accessories like scarves and gloves for a complete outfit look.
Themed gift sets tailored for special occasions like holidays or birthdays, offering curated collections at a discount.
</li>

<li>
<h3>Hold Flash Sales</h3>

Flash sales are an impactful Black Friday promotion idea that creates urgency, driving immediate action from shoppers. These time-limited deals, often available for just a few hours, keep customers excited throughout the day. 

To maximize the effectiveness, leverage social media by posting timely updates on platforms like Instagram or Facebook. These posts remind your followers of upcoming flash sales, ensuring they don’t miss out on exclusive discounts.
</li>

<li>
<h3>Offering Gift Cards</h3>

An highly effective Black Friday marketing idea is to <a href="https://www.99minds.io/giftcard">offer gift cards</a> at a discounted price, such as a $100 gift card for just $85. This tactic is perfect for the holiday season when consumers are actively looking for gifts, ensuring that they will return to make a future purchase. 

This strategy serves two objectives:

<ul>
<li>Encourages Repeat Business: Offering discounted gift cards ensures customers will return for a future purchase, which strengthens engagement and long-term customer relationships.</li>
<li>Immediate Value Appeal: By providing immediate savings (like offering a $100 gift card for $85), you tap into customers' desire for great deals, making them more likely to act quickly and make a purchase.</li>
</ul>

Not only does this give shoppers the perception of getting more value, but customers also often spend more than the gift card’s value when redeeming it, boosting overall sales. Additionally, unredeemed gift cards benefit your business's bottom line.
</li>

<li>
<h3>Start Interactive Social Media Challenges</h3>

Maximize the impact of Black Friday sale ideas by launching interactive social media challenges where participants can win exclusive discounts or freebies. Encourage followers to share your posts, create a Black Friday wishlist with your products, or post photos featuring your brand. 

Consider these tips:
<ul>
<li>Create a branded hashtag to encourage user-generated content.</li>
<li>Engage with participants by replying to comments and shares.</li>
<li>Collaborate with influencers for broader visibility.</li>
<li>Use live videos to interact with followers during the challenge.</li>
<li>Promote the challenge across all your platforms for consistency.</li>
<li>Track performance using analytics to evaluate success.</li>
<li>Offer prizes that resonate with your audience and brand to boost participation.</li>
</ul>
</li>

<li><h3>Collaborate with a cause (Non-profit)</h3>

Aligning your Black Friday sale with a charitable cause can enhance your brand's image and give customers an added reason to purchase. Consider partnering with a charitable organization and pledge a portion of your sales to the cause. This feel-good factor can sway buyers who are on the fence. Promote the partnership leading up to Black Friday, sharing stories about the charity to build an emotional connection. This Black Friday promotion idea blends commerce with purpose, creating a campaign that resonates on a deeper level with your audience.

</li>
<img loading="lazy" role="img" src="/assets/blog/m-s-breast-cancer-now-charity-partnership.jpg" alt="non-profit-collab">

<li><h3>Exclusive member sales</h3>

If you have a membership or subscription model, offering exclusive Black Friday deals or extra discounts can significantly boost <a href="https://www.99minds.io/blog/customer-loyalty" role="link" target="_blanck">customer loyalty</a>. This strategy rewards your most dedicated customers while enticing potential subscribers to join. 

By showcasing the unique perks members receive, like exclusive discounts, you not only encourage current subscribers to maintain their memberships but also create a sense of urgency for new customers to join. 

This Black Friday promotion idea strengthens loyalty, guarantees repeat revenue, and serves as a powerful marketing tool to attract new subscribers.
</li>

<li>
<h3>Implement Augmented Reality (AR) Experience</h3>

Incorporating AR technology into your Black Friday marketing strategy is a game-changer for creating unique shopping experiences. Fashion retailers can implement virtual try-on sessions, allowing customers to digitally try on outfits, reducing returns and enhancing satisfaction. 

For other industries, AR can be used for immersive product demonstrations—such as virtually placing furniture in a customer’s living space or showcasing toys in action. These Black Friday promotion ideas elevate customer engagement, generate buzz, and provide an innovative edge that sets your brand apart.

</li>
<img loading="lazy" role="img" src="/assets/blog/Augmented-Reality.jpeg" alt="augmented-reality-fashion">

<li>
<h3>Offer Referral Discounts</h3>

Building trust and loyalty with your customers is one of the most powerful assets for retailers. A great Black Friday marketing idea is to capitalize on this trust through referral discounts. 

<a href="https://www.99minds.io/blog/referral-marketing" role="link" target="_blanck">Referral-based promotions</a> work by rewarding loyal customers for referring new buyers. For example, when an existing customer brings in a new one who makes a Black Friday purchase, the referrer earns a special discount on their next order. 

This strategy helps expand your customer base organically and incentivizes repeat purchases, turning word-of-mouth into a major sales boost.
</li>

<li>
<h3>Offer Extended Shopping Hours or Early Access</h3>

Extending shopping hours can be a simple yet effective strategy to draw in more foot traffic to your brick-and-mortar store. Opening early or staying open later can accommodate customers with varying schedules. 

For online platforms, offering “early access” to special deals can create a sense of exclusivity and build excitement. Promoting these extended hours or exclusive windows in advance through your social media, email campaigns, or website ensures that your audience is well-informed and ready to take advantage of the opportunity.consistent experience.
</li>

<li>
<h3>Hold an Interactive Live-Stream Shopping Event</h3>

Leverage the power of real-time digital interaction by hosting a live-stream shopping event on platforms like Facebook or Instagram during Black Friday. These events allow you to showcase products, provide live demonstrations, and answer questions on the spot.

 Adding exclusive flash deals for live viewers enhances engagement. The key is to make the session interactive and entertaining, using influencers or knowledgeable staff to host. 
 
 By incorporating live-stream events into your Black Friday marketing ideas, you create a personalized, engaging shopping experience that boosts sales and strengthens customer relationships.
 </li>

 <li>
 <h3>Make a “Black Friday exclusive” post</h3>

Leveraging Facebook's dynamic marketing tools is a game-changer for your Black Friday sales strategy. A powerful way to captivate your audience is by creating a visually enticing “Special Black Friday” carousel ad or post. 

This format allows you to showcase a range of your best sale items in a highly interactive manner, grabbing attention while keeping the focus on your offers.

But it’s not just about aesthetics—creating a sense of urgency is key. Highlight that these deals are limited-time or exclusive to Black Friday, leveraging the psychological principle of scarcity. 

This tactic prompts quick decisions, ensuring your audience doesn’t miss out on your offers. By driving urgency, you can keep your brand and deals at the forefront of shoppers' minds as the big day approaches.

Tips to Maximize Your Black Friday Facebook Carousel Post:

<ul>
<li>High-quality visuals: Use clear, professional images that showcase your products effectively and grab attention.</li>
<li>Compelling headlines: Write short, catchy headlines that emphasize value and urgency, such as “Limited Time Only!” or “Exclusive Black Friday Deal!”.</li>
<li>Clear calls to action (CTAs): Use CTAs like “Shop Now,” “Grab the Deal,” or “Don’t Miss Out” to encourage immediate engagement.</li>
<li>Incorporate customer reviews: Build trust and credibility by including testimonials or user feedback about your products.</li>
<li>Mobile optimization: Ensure your carousel post is mobile-friendly, as the majority of Facebook users access the platform through their phones.</li>

</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="Black-Friday-Marketing-Strategy-Examples-by-Leading-Retailers">Black Friday Marketing Strategy Examples by Leading Retailers</h2>


### MeUndies

MeUndies took a unique and highly effective approach to Black Friday marketing by hosting an exclusive Facebook Live event. This event attracted more than 13,000 viewers and achieved an impressive conversion rate of 25%, far surpassing their usual performance on the platform. 

MeUndies combined entertainment with promotions by featuring a live DJ and dance contests during the event, keeping the audience engaged while unlocking progressively larger discounts as more viewers tuned in.

The campaign was successful not only in terms of sales but also in generating significant word-of-mouth buzz. While MeUndies initially promoted the event to their 300,000 Facebook followers, news of the event quickly spread, reaching a staggering 660,000 people organically. 

This live event became a masterclass in how to use live streaming and real-time audience engagement as part of a Black Friday campaign.

The results of this strategy show how integrating entertainment and interaction with exclusive offers can drive conversions while also building brand awareness. MeUndies' Facebook Live campaign is a prime example of how brands can creatively use digital channels to enhance the Black Friday shopping experience and drive sales.


<img loading="lazy" role="img" src="/assets/blog/meundies.jpeg" alt="meundies-black-friday">


### 22 Days Nutrition's Random Discount Codes</h3>

22 Days Nutrition's Black Friday marketing campaign used a clever gamification strategy to engage shoppers and drive traffic. They introduced randomly generated discount codes to their subscribers, offering discounts ranging from 20% to 50%. 

This added an element of excitement and curiosity, as customers were eager to discover how much of a discount they would receive.

The randomization of discounts played into the psychology of surprise and anticipation, motivating people to visit the website just to "unlock" their savings. 

As a result, 22 Days Nutrition saw a spike in web traffic during their Black Friday sale, as shoppers were drawn to see how much they could save.

This strategy not only created a fun and engaging experience but also enhanced the brand’s visibility and customer retention by providing a unique interaction with the discounting process. 

Moreover, gamified tactics like this encourage repeat visits as customers may want to try their luck again, ensuring steady traffic throughout the Black Friday period.

<img loading="lazy" role="img" src="/assets/blog/22-days-nutrition-black-friday-email.jpeg" style="width: 800px; height: 600px;" alt="22-days-nutrition-black-friday">


### Sugarbones' Mystery Box

Sugarbones, a brand known for its vibrant and empowering designs, executed a highly successful Black Friday marketing strategy centered around the excitement of mystery boxes. 

Led by Cheyenne Federiconi, the founder and artist behind the brand, Sugarbones' approach during Black Friday revolved around creating a unique shopping experience for customers without heavily discounting the entire store.

The mystery box concept played a key role in driving engagement and sales. By offering customers a box filled with surprise items such as enamel pins, stickers, patches, and miniature art prints, Sugarbones created a sense of anticipation and excitement. 

As Cheyenne explained, the mystery box felt like a present, tapping into the joy of receiving something unexpected. This approach was not only a great way to clear out inventory but also a clever method to offer value without undercutting the prices of individual products.

In addition to the mystery boxes, Sugarbones added bonus items to orders placed during the Black Friday and Cyber Monday period, enhancing the customer experience further. 

The brand’s strategy focused on creating fun and memorable shopping experiences rather than relying solely on steep discounts. 

By doing so, Sugarbones was able to maintain its brand value while still offering customers an irresistible deal. 

<img loading="lazy" role="img" src="/assets/blog/sugarbones.jpeg" alt="sugarbones-mystery-box-black-friday">


### Polysleep’s Mock “Leaked Email” Campaign

Polysleep's Black Friday marketing strategy involved a creative and playful approach with their mock “leaked email” campaign. The campaign was designed to intrigue customers by sending out an "accidental" email that revealed upcoming Black Friday deals. 

This tactic generated excitement and curiosity among subscribers, leading to increased engagement. The clever "leak" felt authentic, which helped foster a more personal connection between the brand and its audience.

By using this strategy, Polysleep created a sense of exclusivity and urgency, encouraging customers to take part in their Black Friday sales. The results were impressive, as the brand managed to capture the attention of shoppers in a highly competitive retail environment, standing out with its unconventional and fun marketing approach. This campaign humanized the brand, showcasing its creative side, and ultimately helped boost both engagement and sales during the Black Friday
