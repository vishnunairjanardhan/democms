### <H2><Strong>How to Create a Loyalty Program in Shopify for 2024:</Strong></H2>

### <h3><Strong>Have Clear Objectives:</Strong></h3>

Just like any other marketing campaign, your loyalty program must have clear objectives. Otherwise, you will only be shooting in the dark. You can have a variety of goals for the program. It could be to grow your customer base, increase loyalty, create goodwill for the brand, reduce marketing costs, increase customer lifetime value, or increase engagement. Whatever it is, be careful that you don’t have too many objectives as it can derail your efforts. Your Shopify loyalty program success has a huge bearing on this. 

### <h3><Strong>Choose the Type of Loyalty Program You Want:</Strong></h3>

There are a variety of loyalty programs that you can choose from. Let’s look at some of the options at your disposal.

A. <b>Tiered Loyalty Programs:</b> Here, the customers progress through several levels of tiers, either based on their spending value or the fee associated with becoming a member.

B. <b>Spend-Based Program:</b> Customers get loyalty points based on the amount of money they have spent with your brand. If the loyalty perks are attractive, this can also increase the average order value and even reduce customer churn.

C. <b>VIP Membership Program:</b> In this type of program, customers must pay to be a part of the loyalty program. It gives them exclusive access to certain perks. It could be in the form of discounts, early product access, additional services, etc. 

D. <b>Value-Based Program:</b> The perks for customers in this type of loyalty program will not be a reward for them. It could be in the form of a charity which the Shopify store will donate on their behalf. Customers who are not attracted to discounts, but care about the environment or climate change, might want to associate with businesses that share the same values. 

E. <b>Points Program:</b> This is the most used loyalty program. Here, customers get points based on the value of their purchase. The points can be redeemed based on how much has accumulated. 

F. <b>Gamified Program:</b> Easily one of the most popular types of loyalty programs, here, your loyalty program acts like a gamified version. It helps in encouraging repeat purchases, engages customers, increases loyalty, and creates a great image.

### Flipkart Plus

<a href="https://www.flipkart.com/">Flipkart</a> is an Indian company acquired by Walmart, making it the largest online retailer in the country. The company has its own product line called DigiFlip, featuring laptop bags, tablets, and USB flash drives. Of course, they also sell a wide range of products online, which translates to about <a href="https://www.flipkart.com/">$5 billion in revenue in 2021.</a>

In 2018, Flipkart officially launched its own <a href="https://www.flipkart.com/plus">loyalty system, Flipkart Plus</a>. It’s a no-subscription fee and points-based program for regular customers, who earn points with every purchase. They can get four SuperCoins for every 100 Indian Rupees ($1.32) spent at the Flipkart store. To join the Plus Membership program, customers need to purchase goods in the last 12 months and earn 200 SuperCoins.

Then, members of the Flipkart Plus loyalty program can enjoy:

<ul>
<li>Free and fast shipping.</li>
<li>Early access to significant sale events and an ever-expanding list of services.</li>
<li>A superior customer support experience.</li>
</ul>

Also, Flipkart Plus members can earn double SuperCoin, enjoy unique Flipkart Pay Later options, and benefit from ecosystem partners in travel, lifestyle, health, and fitness. Each purchase is limited to 100 coins and has a one-year expiration date.

This loyalty system has been a huge success and achieved over 100% year-on-year growth. Around <a href="https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/flipkart-plus-sees-robust-growth-reaches-20-million-customer-base-121081901007_1.html">30% of Flipkart’s monthly active customers are now Plus members</a>. On top of that, members, on average, <a href="https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/flipkart-plus-sees-robust-growth-reaches-20-million-customer-base-121081901007_1.html">transact 5X more and have 7X higher spending</a> than the rest of the shoppers. Currently, Flipkart issues more than 1 billion SuperCoins per month, allowing customers to enjoy various benefits. That sounds like a successful customer loyalty program, doesn’t it?
