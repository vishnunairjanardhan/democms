<h2 id="How-do-Shopify-Gift-Cards-Work?">How do Shopify Gift Cards Work?</h2>
<ul role= "list">
<li><b>Purchase:</b> Customers can purchase Shopify gift cards issued in the Shopify store or from merchants using the Shopify platform. These gift cards can be physical or email codes sent to the recipient.</li>
<li><b>Redemption:</b> Beneficiaries can redeem the gift card's value at checkout at any store that accepts it as payment. They only accept specialty gift cards sent via mail or email.</li>
<li><b>Usage:</b> The Shopify gift card's value is subtracted from the total purchase amount. If the purchase exceeds the gift card amount, the buyer may pay the balance using another valid payment method.</li>
<li><b>Balance check:</b> Beneficiaries can check their gift cards through the card issuer or by entering the gift card on the store's website.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id= "Difference-Between-Adding-to-a-Gift-Card-vs.-Creating-a-Gift-Card"> Difference Between Adding to a Gift Card vs. Creating a Gift Card</h2>

1. <h3>Create an account on Shopify</h3>

You must have a live Shopify account to sell gift cards on the platform. This is how to begin:
<ul role= "list">
<li><b>Register:</b> Visit Shopify's website to create an account. Inputting your store name, setting up a password, and supplying an email address are required.</li>
<li><b>Select a Plan:</b> Choose a pricing plan based on your requirements. Shopify provides a variety of plans, each with unique features.</li>
<li><b>Setup Your Store:</b> Enter your store details, including your address, currency, and time zone, by following the setup instructions. Tailor the theme and style of your store to reflect your brand.</li>
</ul>

2. <h3>Add vs. Create Share Their Difference</h3>
To manage your store effectively, you must know the difference between adding and creating gift cards in Shopify.
<ul role= "list">
<li><b>Add Gift Cards:</b> Adding gift cards usually means turning on your Shopify store's gift card feature. This entails setting up things that people can buy with gift cards. How to add gift cards is as follows:</li>
<ol role= "list">
<li><b>Go to Products:</b> Select Gift Cards under Products in your Shopify admin.</li>
<li><b>Activate Gift Cards:</b> To sell gift cards, click the link. You might need to update your Shopify plan to one that accepts gift cards if you can't see this option.</li>
<li><b>Add a Product Gift Card:</b> To add a gift card product, click Add. Add the required information, including the image upload, denominations (values), title, and description.</li>
<li><b>Save and Release:</b> Once you have saved your edits, publish the gift card product. Customers will be able to buy it from now on.</li>
</ol>
</ul>

3. <h3>Create Gift Cards</h3>
In order to create gift cards, gift cards must be manually issued for customer service or promotional purposes. Straight from the Shopify admin is where you do this. This is how one makes a gift card:
<ul role= "list">
<li><b>Go to Products:</b> Go to Gift Cards under Products.</li>
<li><b>Give out gift cards:</b> Select "Gift card issue" by clicking.</li>
<li><b>Add Information:</b> Enter the gift card's information, such as the starting value, the recipient's details, and any personalized notes. If necessary, you can also define an expiration date.</li>
<li><b>Save and Send:</b> After entering all the information, save the gift card. You can email the gift card's unique code which Shopify will generate to the recipient.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id= "How-to-Get-Started-with-Shopify-Gift-Cards?"> How to Get Started with Shopify Gift Cards?</h2>
