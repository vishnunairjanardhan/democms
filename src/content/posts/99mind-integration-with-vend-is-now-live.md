## You can now accept Vend gift cards on Bigcommerce!

In the last few months, we have been tirelessly working to improve our app features, and we believe that this newest feature will help you enjoy the Gift Card experience even more.

## So what is this new feature all about?

It wasn’t possible to redeem Vend gift cards online until recently. There was a time when having a Vend gift card meant that you could redeem it only in-store through Vend directly.
But now, Using the 99minds gift card app on Vend, you will be able to redeem vend Giftcards on any Bigcommerce store!. <a href="https://www.zendesk.com/help-center-closed/?utm_source=helpcenter-closed&utm_medium=poweredbyzendesk&utm_campaign=text&utm_content=99minds.zendesk.com" target="_blank"> Read more</a>
