Direct-to-consumer (DTC) brands often face a common challenge: turning product returns into repeat purchases instead of losing revenue. Every return represents more than a refund; it can weaken <a href="https://www.99minds.io/blog/customer-loyalty" role="link" target="_blanck">customer loyalty</a>. 

Implementing store credit helps you change that outcome. When you convert refunds into credits that customers can redeem in your store, you keep them engaged and ensure the money stays within your brand’s ecosystem.

Research shows that returning customers drive <a href="https://www.gorgias.com/playbooks/cx-growth" role="link" target="_blanck">44% of total revenue</a>, even though they make up a smaller portion of your overall customer base. This shows that every effort to bring customers back, including offering store credit, directly supports long-term profitability.

Instead of treating returns as the end of the buying journey, use store credit to rebuild trust, encourage future purchases, and strengthen customer loyalty.

<h2 id="What-is-Store-Credit?">What is Store Credit?</h2>

Store credit is a digital balance or virtual currency provided to customers, often as a refund alternative or reward. Unlike cash refunds, store credit is redeemable exclusively within the issuing store. It effectively keeps the money in your ecosystem while giving customers an incentive to return.

Store credit can be issued for a variety of reasons:

- Refund replacements
- Compensation for mistakes or delays
- Customer loyalty rewards
- <a href="https://www.99minds.io/blog/gift-card-promotions" role="link" target="_blanck">Gift card promotions</a>

By keeping funds within your store, store credit turns what might be a loss into a potential revenue opportunity.

<h2 id="How-Does-Store-Credit-Work?">How Does Store Credit Work?</h2>

When retailers offer store credit, they provide customers with a specific dollar amount that can be used for future purchases within their business. This online store credit acts as an internal currency, ensuring that the funds stay within the retailer's ecosystem rather than being refunded back to a bank account.

What is store credit exactly? It's not just a simple refund; it represents a more strategic approach to <a href="https://www.99minds.io/blog/customer-retention" role="link" target="_blanck">customer retention</a>. Unlike traditional refunds, store credit is not transferable across different stores or brands. This exclusivity benefits both the customer and the retailer: customers receive a flexible yet dedicated spending option, and retailers keep the funds within their business, potentially boosting future sales.

Store credit can come in various forms, such as gift cards, loyalty rewards, or store-specific credit cards. These forms are typically exclusive to the issuing store, and in many cases, they do not expire, offering long-term value to customers. This system is particularly effective in <a href="https://www.99minds.io/blog/build-customer-loyalty" role="link" target="_blanck">building customer loyalty</a> and encouraging <a href="https://www.99minds.io/blog/repeat-customer" role="link" target="_blanck">repeat business</a>, as it simplifies the exchange process for returns and transforms potential losses from returns into future sales opportunities.

By using store credit and online <a href="https://www.99minds.io/store-credit" role="link" target="_blanck">store credit systems</a>, businesses can enhance their customer service experience, offering a tangible incentive that encourages customers to continue shopping with them, thereby solidifying customer loyalty and improving overall satisfaction.

<h2 id="What-are-the-Benefits-of-Issuing-Online-Store-Credit?">What are the Benefits of Issuing Online Store Credit?</h2>

Offering store credit is a strategic approach adopted by many of the world's top retailers because it enhances flexibility and fosters customer loyalty and spending. Here are three main advantages of implementing store credit, especially in the form of online store credit, as opposed to providing cash refunds:
<ol>
<li>
<h3>Enhance Customer Retention and Loyalty</h3>

Store credit acts as a powerful incentive for customers to return to your store. As a key component of a comprehensive <a href="https://www.99minds.io/blog/loyalty-program" role="link" target="_blanck">customer loyalty program</a>, online store credit demonstrates to customers the value of their continued patronage. 

For instance, a hypothetical retailer, <a href="https://ecostyleco.org/" role="link" target="_blanck">EcoStyle Apparel</a>, uses store credit to encourage customers to invest in sustainable fashion choices. By returning items, customers receive credit that they can use to continue supporting eco-friendly fashion without immediate cash expenditure.
</li>

<li>
<h3>Encourage Increased Spending</h3>

While it might seem paradoxical, store credit can actually lead customers to spend more than they originally planned. Similar to the psychological spending boost observed with credit card usage, customers with store credit feel more liberated to exceed the credit amount. For example, a home goods store might issue $30 in store credit, which customers often use as a base, adding additional funds to purchase higher-value items like luxury bedding or high-end kitchen gadgets.
</li>

<li>
<h3>Minimize Revenue Loss Due to Returns</h3>

Returns are a necessary part of retail that can complicate revenue streams. Store credit mitigates these losses by transforming potential return transactions into exchanges. This is particularly effective in industries like electronics, where customers might return a gadget due to preference rather than functionality. 

By offering store credit, businesses like <a href="https://everdion.com/" role="linl" target="_blanck">Everdion</a> encourage customers to choose an alternative product rather than seeking a refund, thus retaining sales within the company. Understanding what is store credit and its implications can greatly benefit both retailers and customers
</li>

<li>
<h3>Keep Cash in Business with Gift Cards</h3>

Store credit helps to keep the cash in business. By offering store credit or gift cards instead of cash refunds you maintain the cash flow in your business. The best tactic to use is to <a href="https://www.99minds.io/blog/gift-card-promotions" role="link" target="_blanck">offer gift cards</a> of values higher than the amount of the refund hence it entices customers to spend more.

<h2 id="How-to-use-Store-Credit-to-Increase-Customer-Retention">How to use Store Credit to Increase Customer Retention</h2>

There are various strategies your store can employ to leverage online store credit effectively, and here are three significant methods:
<ol>
<li>
<h3>Be Charitable with Store Credit</h3>

According to Nielsen, six out of ten consumers now prefer to purchase from brands that are sustainable or socially responsible. For many DTC brands, contributing to meaningful causes can be financially challenging. That’s where online store credit can be an innovative solution.

Instead of cash-based donations, you can use store credit as a form of charitable giving, helping communities while keeping resources within your brand’s ecosystem.

This could involve:

- Providing online store credit to employees, members, or beneficiaries of non-profits.
- Donating gift cards or digital credit to charities that align with your brand values.
- Allowing customers to vote at checkout for a non-profit to receive a monthly online store credit donation.

Offering store credit instead of cash donations is a more feasible way for smaller retailers to give back, while also increasing brand goodwill and customer loyalty.
</li>

<li>
<h3>Incentivize Referrals</h3>

Take inspiration from the success of direct-to-consumer brands like <a href="https://rothys.com/" role="link" target="blanck">Rothy’s</a> and <a href="https://www.warbyparker.com/" role="link" target="blanck">Warby Parker</a>, which have grown significantly through innovative <a href="https://www.99minds.io/blog/referral-program" role="link" target="blanck">referral programs</a>. 

Encourage your customers to promote your brand by offering online store credit for referrals that convert into new sales. This strategy not only drives new <a href="https://www.99minds.io/blog/customer-acquisition" role="link" target="_blanck">customer acquisition</a> but also enhances loyalty among existing customers. 

As suggested by industry experts, providing perks like bonus points or discounts on future purchases with online store credit can effectively motivate customers to prefer store credit options.
</li>

<div class="m-auto w-full lg:px-34">
<img loading="lazy" role="img" src="/assets/blog/Screenshot 2024-07-05 183519.webp" alt="blume referral program">
</div>

<li>
<h3>Expand Your Return Policy</h3>

An expansive return policy can significantly influence purchasing decisions. Statistics show that two-thirds of consumers review a retailer’s return policy before buying, and over half are deterred by unsatisfactory policies. 

By offering online store credit for returns, you can introduce more flexibility into your return policy without losing revenue. This approach allows for extended return periods and fewer restrictions on how returns are made (such as needing original tags or receipts), making customers feel more secure and likely to make purchases.
</li>

<li>
<h3>Encourage Future Purchases with Time-Limited Offers</h3>

Make your online store credit more appealing by attaching time-sensitive offers to it. For example, offering a bonus credit if the store credit is used within a certain period can accelerate the return of customers to your store, prompting quicker and potentially more frequent purchases.
</li>

<li>
<h3>Offer Store Credit for Customer Reviews</h3>

Use online store credit as an incentive for customers to leave reviews on your products or services. This not only can improve the quality and quantity of your reviews but also encourages customers to engage with your brand and make subsequent purchases using the credit they earn.
</li>

<li>
<h3>Enhance Personalized Marketing</h3>

Personalize marketing campaigns based on the customer’s previous purchases and offer tailored store credit deals that match their buying behavior. For instance, if a customer frequently buys books from a specific genre, offer them store credit that can be used towards the next purchase in that same genre.
</li>

<li>
<h3>Implement a Graduated Rewards System</h3>

Encourage more spending by implementing a graduated rewards system where the value of the store credit increases with the amount spent. This method encourages larger purchases, as customers see the direct benefits of spending more.
</li>

<li>
<h3>Utilize Store Credit for Customer Milestones</h3>

Acknowledge customer loyalty by offering store credit on special milestones, such as the anniversary of their first purchase, their birthday, or after a set number of purchases. This not only delights customers but also reinforces their good feelings about your brand, boosting what is store credit used for and how it can benefit them.
</li>
</ol>
