<h2 id="What-is-Shopify-Store-Credit?">What is Shopify Store Credit?</h2>

Shopify Store credit represents a value that retailers provide to customers as an alternative to traditional cash refunds. Since this credit must be used at the same store, it helps retain funds within the business whenever products are returned. Store credit is not equivalent to cash; it is only valid at the retailer that issues it. However, it holds a value that can be used like cash to purchase items at that specific store.

If you are a store owner, imagine your customer buys a pair of sneakers from your online boutique, but they don't fit them when they arrive. Instead of a cash refund, your store can issue them store credit for the amount of the purchase. Next time they shop with you, they can select a new pair or any other product and use their shopify store credits at checkout, only paying any additional cost if the new item is more expensive.

<h2 id="How-Does-Store-Credit-Work?">How Does Store Credit Work?</h2>

When retailers issue store credit, they provide customers with a designated dollar amount usable for future purchases exclusively within their business. This shopify store credit serves as internal currency, keeping the funds within the retailer's ecosystem instead of returning them to a bank account.

So is store credit the same as a refund? It's more than just a simple refund; it's a strategic approach to boosting customer retention. Unlike standard refunds, shopify store credit is not transferable across different stores or brands, enhancing its value both to the customer and the retailer. This exclusivity offers customers a flexible spending option and helps retailers retain funds, potentially increasing future sales.

Forms of store credit include gift cards, loyalty rewards, or store-specific credit cards. These are generally exclusive to the issuing store and often do not expire, providing long-term value to customers. <h2 id="How-to-Issue-Shopify-Store-Credit?">How to Issue Shopify Store Credit?</h2>

Let's explore how you can issue shopify store credit to both existing customers and potential customers:
<ol>
<li>
<h3>Use Discount Codes to Issue Shopify Store Credit</h3>
<ul>
<li><b>Log in to your Shopify Admin:</b> Access your Shopify dashboard by logging into your account.</li>
<li><b>Access the Customer's Profile:</b> Navigate to the "Customers" section in your admin panel and search for the customer by name or email.</li>
<li><b>Edit Customer Details:</b> Click on the customer's name to open their profile.</li>
<li><b>Add a Note:</b> In the "Notes" section, mention the reason for issuing the store credit and specify the amount.
Issue Store Credit: Use the "Tags" section to add a "Store Credit" tag, making it easier to track customers with store credits.</li>
<li><b>Apply a Discount Code:</b> Create a unique discount code equivalent to the store credit amount under the "Discounts" section of your Shopify admin. Provide this code to the customer.</li>
<li><b>Notify the Customer:</b> Inform the customer about their store credit, providing them with the discount code and instructions on how to use it.</li>
</ul>
</li>

<li>
<h3>Use Gift Cards to Issue Shopify Store Credit</h3>

<ul>
<li><b>Log in to Shopify:</b>  Start by accessing your Shopify account.</li>
<li><b>Navigate to Gift Cards:</b> Go to "Products" > "Gift Cards."</li>
<li><b>Issue a Gift Card:</b> Select "Issue gift card" within the Gift Cards section.</li>
<li><b>Customize the Gift Card:</b> Set the details for the gift card, including the recipient's information, and activate it.</li>
<li><b>Notify the Customer:</b> Let the customer know they have received a gift card, which they can use like regular currency in your store.</li>
</ul>
</li>

<li>
<h3>Issue store credit from your Shopify admin</h3>

<b>Enable store credit at checkout:</b> Enabling store credit at checkout provides a seamless way for customers to utilize their credits when making purchases. During the checkout process, once a customer logs into their account, they will see an option to apply any available store credit towards their current order. 
Store credit in Shopify can be efficiently managed directly from a customer’s profile in the Shopify admin. Here, you can easily credit and debit store credit as needed. It's also possible to view the current balance of store credit a customer holds, which can be displayed in multiple currencies.

When issuing store credit from a customer's profile:
<ul>
<li>You can specify the amount to be credited.</li>
<li>You have the option to set an expiration date for the credit.</li>
</ul>

Store credit can also be manually reduced by debiting the balance from the customer's profile. When a customer uses their store credit or when adjustments are made manually, the order of usage is based on the expiration dates, with credits expiring the soonest being used first.
</li>
