## Benefits of Scheduling

Digital or eGift cards make incredible last-minute gifts because they can be delivered almost instantly. Sometimes, however, you want to order the gift in advance but hold delivery until a more appropriate moment–like Christmas Eve, Christmas morning, a Birthday, an Anniversary, or some other memorable date on the calendar. While you could get up early that day and just hit send, scheduling egift card delivery is more comfortable, and it reduces the possibility of forgetting to ship a gift altogether.

There are plenty of designs and ways to schedule an eGift card delivery on all online stores using the <a href="https://www.99minds.io/" target="_blank">99Minds app</a>, so its arrival will be timed correctly. Just think how efficient you could be this holiday season! Early in the month, spend a few minutes ordering and scheduling eGift card deliveries from the merchants above. Then sit back and spend the rest of the season relaxing, knowing your gifts are loaded into Santa’s Cyber-sleigh and will shortly be on their way.
