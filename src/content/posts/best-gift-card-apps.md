Rise.ai is a popular solution for Shopify brands looking to add advanced gifting capabilities. It’s known for store credit, automated workflows, and elegant customer experiences.

Shopify DTC brands needing strong automation for gift cards and store credit.

**Key Features**

- Store credit and rewards
- Customizable branded gift cards

- Gift card reminders and gifting options
